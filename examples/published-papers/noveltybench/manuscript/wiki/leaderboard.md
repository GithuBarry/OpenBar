# Leaderboard

Site: https://novelty-bench.github.io/
Harness: https://github.com/novelty-bench/novelty-bench

A submission is `evaluation/<date>_<model>/{nb-curated,nb-wildchat}/{scores.jsonl,summary.json}` plus `paper` and `model` URLs.

In scope: training or inference-time methods on openly specified models. Closed products and unreproducible self-reports are out of scope.

Paper Table 1 is the COLM 2025 freeze. New rows go on the site, not back into the camera-ready table.
